04 · The production run, epoch by epoch

plates_y11n5 — 250 epochs planned, 100-epoch early-stopping patience. It stopped at epoch 150 after 100 epochs with no further gain; Ultralytics had already saved its best snapshot back at epoch 116. Running on to 150 instead of stopping there cost extra wall-clock time for no benefit, but did no harm — Ultralytics always keeps the best checkpoint regardless. 06 · Checkpoint to phone

None of the above ships as-is. A checkpoint is exported to TensorFlow Lite with NMS baked in, and only that file travels any further:

yolo export model=best.pt format=tflite imgsz=640 nms=True conf=0.25 iou=0.45 max_det=300

The resulting best_float16.tflite — a few megabytes, not the dataset or the checkpoints behind it — is copied into the Android app's assets and picked up by an on-device TFLite interpreter at runtime. The training run, this page's raw data, and every intermediate checkpoint stay local and out of version control.

Verification at this stage is manual, not a benchmark suite: rebuild android-end-user-app with the new checkpoint in its assets, install it on a device, and check detection by eye against real frames. That's the extent of "done" for now — there's no automated on-device regression suite yet.

07 · What's next

This checkpoint is a bootstrap, not the destination.

The collection and reviewing apps have since produced a second, much more targeted batch of curated uploads — real Ukrainian plates captured and corrected through this project's own pipeline, instead of a generic public dataset. The infrastructure for that loop is already built and working end to end; what's missing is time spent consolidating those packages into one clean training set (dedup, split, sanity-check labels), which is a deliberate manual step today (see datasets/). That consolidation, not compute or tooling, is the actual bottleneck before the next training run.

Real-world, on-device examples of what this checkpoint catches and misses belong here too — to be added once there's field evidence to show.

Plate Detector is a portfolio project: a full ML product loop, from a data-collection app through human review to this training pipeline and a published, network-free Android detector.
